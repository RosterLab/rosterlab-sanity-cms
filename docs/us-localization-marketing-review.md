## Priority 1: align outcome claims with the evidence

### Modeled versus achieved savings

Sanity slug: `how-plastics-department-used-roster-simulation-to-cut-in-costs`.
The title says the plastics unit used simulation to “Cut 15% in Costs.” The body
says the proposed model **would** generate approximately 15% savings. The supplied
body establishes a forecast, not independently verified realized savings.

Suggested US title, unless achieved results are substantiated:

> How a Plastics Unit Used Scheduling Simulation to Model 15% Potential Cost Savings

Retain the customer's actual location and the source slug. Check the article
excerpt, case-study gate and other promotional placements for the same implication.

### Whitepaper proof and attribution

The [US whitepaper landing page](../app/us/whitepapers/rostering-as-a-strategic-workforce-lever/page.tsx)
attributes becoming fully staffed to scheduling changes and groups `$800k+`, 93%
less scheduling time and “zero compliance breaches” immediately after an Auckland
example. The cached Auckland case supports estimated `$80,000+`, approximately
93% less scheduling time and zero **MECA-rule** breaches. The PDF or other source
evidence may support additional outcomes; those claims are not disproven by this
audit, but each needs an identified source and its proper scope.

Do not generalize a contractual-rule outcome into broad US regulatory assurance.
Confirm the previously flagged source currencies before adding currency labels.
A narrower evidence-backed direction for the Auckland example is:

> An Auckland radiology department reports reducing monthly scheduling time from
> 120 hours to 8 hours, while meeting more than 600 staff preferences per
> scheduling period.

### Product compliance claims

`optimizing-on-call-scheduling` claims RosterLab tracks FLSA, FMLA, OSHA and other
rules and makes schedules legally compliant. `2-2-3-panama-shift-scheduling`
promises “fully compliant” schedules. Verify these product capabilities and the
scope of the claims with the product owner. Merely using American terminology
does not substantiate these promises.

Subject to product confirmation, a narrower direction is to explain configuring
team requirements and reviewing generated schedules against applicable policies
and agreements. This review does not determine legal obligations or certify
compliance.
